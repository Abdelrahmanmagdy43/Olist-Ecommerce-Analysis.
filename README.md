Olist E-commerce Performance Dashboard (Power BI)                   ![Olist Dashboard](Olist%20Project%20Dashboard.jpg)
📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset, focusing on sales performance, customer satisfaction, and payment behavior. The dashboard provides a high-level view of business growth and operational efficiency from 2016 to 2018.

🛠️ Technical Skills
Data Integration: Connecting complex relational datasets from Kaggle.

Power Query: Cleaning and transforming data for accurate reporting.

Data Visualization: Creating an interactive dark-themed dashboard for better user experience.

💡 Key Insights (From the Dashboard):
Revenue Growth: There is a clear and steady upward trend in revenue from 2016 to 2018, showing consistent business expansion.

Top Market: SP (São Paulo) is significantly the most active state, dominating the "Top Active States" treemap.

Customer Satisfaction: The average review score is 4.09, indicating a generally high level of customer satisfaction across 104K orders.

Payment Preferences: Credit Cards are the most preferred payment method, accounting for the vast majority (73.92%) of transactions.

Top Category: The "beleza_saude" (Health & Beauty) category leads in revenue, contributing approximately $1.26M.
